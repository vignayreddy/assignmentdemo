Community Bulletin

A Letter From the Mayor

Our community continues to grow stronger through active participation and shared values. This summer season brings a renewed focus on public parks, clean energy initiatives, and local neighborhood gatherings. We invite all families to join our weekly outdoor activities and share feedback at city hall.

We are also looking for volunteers to help organize the annual fall parade. If you have skills in event coordination, marketing, or logistics, please stop by our central booth during any featured weekend event to register your interest.
